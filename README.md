## AI-Powered Book Rating using Knowledge Graphs and GNNs

#### Authors: Tikhon Belousov, Michał Głuszkiewicz, Bartosz Burszyk

Have you ever had a problem of choosing which book to read next? Or maybe you were unsure whether the book that you chose is good or not, and you wish there was a system that would tell you the *objective rating*?

Well, there's a high chance that the **Booktaste AI** can help you with that!

Our model takes into account both stylistical and storytelling aspects of literature texts. It evaluates books by first turning them into **Knowledge Graphs** containing book characters and relations between them, and then feeding those knowledge graphs together with stylometric features into a **GNN**. This allows our model to take into account both book's style and narration.
